# Side lunge + rotation

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Open up groin + hip
## Textual Description

- Start kneeling on one leg with the other leg straight off to the side like a triangle
	- The toes should be facing front of you
- put your hands at your hips
- Sit down this should also push your leg further off to the side
- Then move your body forwards and aim your arms forward pushing yourself to the ground
- Do this for 3 sets of 12
