# Kneeling Thoracic Extension

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Helps with optimal posture, deep core + pelvic fore strengths
- Reduces shoulder pain + lower back pain
- Improves spinal curvature
- Improves lung volume
## Textual Description

- Start with your hands at your head and your head elevated above a chair/bench
- Then bring your head down while driving your arms behind your body almost reaching towards your spine
- Should feel a stretch towards your top of your back
