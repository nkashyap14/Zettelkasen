# Face Down Lumbar rotations

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

-  An exercise that primarily works the muscles around the lower back improving flexibility in the lumbar spine by allowing for twisting motion, which can help relieve back pain, enhance posture, and reduce the risk of injury by strengthening the supporting muscles in the area; key muscles involved include the obliques, multifidus, and longissimus thoracis
## Textual Description

- Lay face down
- Move one leg over your butt to the other side floor trying to touch
- Same thing with the other leg
- Keep your arms and shoulders glued to the ground almost
