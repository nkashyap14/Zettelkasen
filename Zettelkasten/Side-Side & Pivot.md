# Side-Side & Pivot

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter how not to get cornered
## Textual Description

- Go to the outside with your lead leg then bring your left leg into the same x axis with your lead leg and shift your weight back to the left leg by bringing yuour lead leg into the left legs position and shifting back
