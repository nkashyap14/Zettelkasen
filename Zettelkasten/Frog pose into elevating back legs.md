# Side lunge + rotation

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Open up groin + hip + increase flexibility for splits
- Stretch hip flexors
## Textual Description

- Start in the frog pose sitting on your back legs
	- Try to point your knees outwards
- Push forwards into the frog pose
- Then lift your back legs one by one
	- While lifting each leg  make sure to try and look at that leg as if to deepen the stretch
- Do 3 sets of 11
