# Forward & Back Footwork

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter how to move his feet quickly
- Slight bend in the knees
- Center of gravity low
- Balance is key. Keep your shoulder over your hips otherwise you will lose balance
## Textual Description

- Start on the outside of the ladder
- Advance at 45 degree angle
- Second leg catches up
- Leg that stepped forward steps out
- Other leg goes forward
- Other leg catches up
- Continue wiht the stepping out of the leg that stepped forward
