# Balance_Power_Technique_Speed Sidekick Drills

## Goal of Drill

- This is a collection of 4 drills that work different aspects of the sidekick
- From balance on the sidekick motion
- To the general technique of the sidekick focusing on a clear chamber
- Enhancing speed of the sidekick by kicking both sides
- And also improving power by starting from kneeling position to a sidekick
