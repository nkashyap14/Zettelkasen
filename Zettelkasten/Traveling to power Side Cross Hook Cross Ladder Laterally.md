# Traveling to power Side Cross Hook Cross Ladder Laterally
## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter the basic movements on how to move to the power side and throw a combo
## Textual Description

- Going through the ladder laterally
- Keep lead leg in the ladder rungs and the rear leg in the rung next to it
- Step to the power side
- Rear leg moves first then lead leg follows
- Throw a 2 3 2
