# Forward & Back Footwork

## Goal of Drill

- To teach beginners brand new to boxing footwork a great way to drill a basic movement pattern
## Textual Description

- Keep lead foot in the ladder
- Back foot outside the ladder in basic stance
- Move forwards and backwards up the ladder
- You're traveling the ladder from end to end not
- Next progression is to make it more dynamic. Focus on pushing off the backleg when moving forward and pushing off the front foot when moving backwards
	- Focus on the spring of the feet
