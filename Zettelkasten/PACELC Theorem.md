# PACELC Theorem

## Definition:

- A more modern theorem than the [[CAP Theorem]] that also talks about what choices does a distributed system have when there are no network partitions
- Says that in the case of a partition a [[Distributed System]] can trade of between [[Availability]] and [[Consistency]] else under normal operating conditions a system must trade off between [[Consistency]] and [[Latency]]
