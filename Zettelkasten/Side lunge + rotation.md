# Side lunge + rotation

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Open up groin + hip
## Textual Description

- Start kneeling on one leg with the other leg straight off to the side like a triangle
	- The toes should be facing away from you of the leg straight off
- put your hands at your sides of the head
- Lean over to the leg that was straight off which then becomes bent at almost a 100 degree angle
- Lean the elbow that is closer to the bent leg ahead of the knee 
- Then when coming back up rotate the other elbow to the front
- Then return to starting position and start again
- Do for 3 sets of 10
