# Continuous Drop & Catch (Tennis Ball Drill)

## Goal of Drill

- Improve eye hand coordination + hand speed + focus
## Textual Description

- Hold two tennis balls in both hand
- let go and cross your arms to catch
- Let go and uncross your arms to catch
- Repeat
