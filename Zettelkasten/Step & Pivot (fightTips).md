# Step & Pivot (fightTips)

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter how to move in a fight specific movement pattern

## Textual Description


- Hands up the entire time. Moving laterally on the laddre
- Step in and step out of a ladder rung
- Then step in again and pivot and then step out
- Then step in at a 45 degree angle and pivot in the opposite direction
- You are alternating pivot directions
	- you're always pivoting towards the direction that the ladder started in
- Theoretically after each pivot there is a bounce out and a bounce back in
- This is an advanced footwork drill
