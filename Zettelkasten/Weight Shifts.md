# Weight Shifts

## Goal of Drill

- To give a fighter the ability to rapidly shift weight and switch stance while also cutting an angle
- Increase footspeed and ability to make rapid changes in feet while keeping balance and weight 
## Textual Description

- Have a leg in the band. Then move the other leg into the band while then also moving that same leg in the band outside the band while keeping stance.
