# 5_5 Maximum Likelihood Estimation

## General Details

- As maximum likelihood is defined in general setup this also extends to estimators for conditional likelihood estimators.
$$
\theta_{ML} = \operatorname*{argmax}_{\theta} \sum_{i=1}^m log [P(y^{(i)} | x^{(i)};\theta)]
$$
