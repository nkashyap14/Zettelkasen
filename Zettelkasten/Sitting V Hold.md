# Side lunge + rotation

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Open up groin + hip + split ability
## Textual Description

- Start sitting with your legs on the ground
- Then bring your feet up while still sitting and grab your feet and pull them outwards
- Hold for 15 seconds for 4 sets
