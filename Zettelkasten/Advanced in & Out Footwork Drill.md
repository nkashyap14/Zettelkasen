# Advanced in & Out Footwork Drill
## Goal of Drill

- To get a fighter familiar with throwing the jab and also jab cross while moving in and out
## Textual Description

- Start by moving in and out of the ladder rung facing laterally. First its just a bounce to feel out rhythm. Do something like 5-10
- Then start throwing jabs when you step in. Do something like 5 stepping jabs
- Then do the same thing bouncing but throw the 1-2 as well. Need to make sure to keep the rhythm as well.
