# Tae Kwan Do

## Summary

- Korean created martial art whose practitioners tend to lack punching ability while also having exceptional footwork and kicking ability along with flexibility. Goal here is to incorporate aspects of taekwando training that can be applicable for a general martial arts practitioner while not giving ground in other aspects of the fight game
