# Intermediate Combination 1 Boxing Ladder Drills
## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter a combo focusing on linear movement that allows them to bait the opponent for a counter with their rear cross
- Good for teaching the fighter to change direction midway through a combination
## Textual Description

- Going through the ladder linearly
- Step forward two rungs throwing a double jab
- Then move backwards one rung
- Throw a cross upon setting feet
- Reset to stance after the pull
