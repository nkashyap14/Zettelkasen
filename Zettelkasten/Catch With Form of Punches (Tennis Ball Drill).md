# Catch With Form of Punches (Tennis Ball Drill)

## Goal of Drill

- Improve eye hand coordination + hand speed + focus
## Textual Description

- Catch tennis balls with the form of punches
- Make sure to rotate your back leg when doing a rear hook or a rear upper
- Can also catch with a lead hook
- Be creative
