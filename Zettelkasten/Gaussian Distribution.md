# Gaussian Distribution

- Standard probability distribution that is parameterized by a mean and variance

$$
p(x^{(i)}; \mu, \sigma^2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}}exp(-\frac{1}{2}\frac{(x^{(i)} - \mu)^2}{\sigma^2})
$$
