# Rotate & Pull Hook Kick

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- The goal of this drill is to increase internal rotation + open up the hips for the hook kick
## Textual Description

- Stand on right leg. Grab something for balance
- Chamber the left leg as in a knee raise a bit in front of you
- Rotate the leg pointing perpendicular away from you then bring it back  so that it opens up your hips
- Do for at least 10 reps
- Can do 3-5 sets
