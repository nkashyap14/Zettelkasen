# Pivot & Pull Hook Kick

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- The goal of this drill is to increase internal rotation + work on the actual pulling motion in the hook kick
## Textual Description

- Stand on right leg. Chamber your leg in front of you
- Movement pattern is similar to the [[Rotate & Pull Hook Kick]] however you do it all in one motion rather than dividing up the rotation and the pull over
- This exercise should be built to. It is the natural progression
