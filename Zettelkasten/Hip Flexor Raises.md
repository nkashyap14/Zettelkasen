# Hip Flexor Raises

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Fire up the hip flexors + increase overall mobility for them.
- Useful for kicks for [[Muay Thai]] and [[Dutch Kickboxing]]
## Textual Description

- Put a kettle bell on the ground. Keep hands by your side and then raise a single leg up and over the kettle bell and then bring it back over the same side
