# Intermediate Combination 2 Boxing Ladder Drills
## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter to throw a combo while focusing on lateral movement and changing up the angle
- Can get creative with the combo
## Textual Description

- Start with the lead leg in a ladder rung and the back leg outside the ladder and behind
- Take a diagonal step, for an [[orthodox]] it would be diagonal to the left for a [[Southpaw]] it would be diagonal to the right
	- With the lead leg
- Back foot comes into the ladder
	- Still keep about one ladder rung apart
- Then throw a combination
- Once combination is done bring yourself back into the ladder by taking a sidestep with the rear leg and then bring the lead leg into the ladder
- Basic combo can be cross hook cross
	- Throw the combo after each of the steps
- Typically  combo could lead with power shots like crosses or hooks
