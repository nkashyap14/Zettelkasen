# One Two Stepping Linearly Ladder

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter the basic movements on how to jab cross while moving linearly forwards and backwards
## Textual Description

- Going through the ladder linearly
- Step with the lead leg as you throw the jab
- Lead hand retracts while you bring the rear leg forward and twist while throwing the rear cross all in one fluid motion
- Return to boxing stance
- Jab should have slight rotation of hips
- Cross should have full rotation of hips and core
