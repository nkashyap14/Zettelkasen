# Switched Steps + Pivots

## Goal of Drill

- To increase ability to stance switch
- To get a faster switch for future feints or even [[switch kicks]]
- To increase ability to switch into a pivot / cut angle
- A switch in this case refers to when you are in orthodox stance and rapidly swithc by bringing right leg forward and left leg back so that you are in southpaw stance
## Textual Description

- Keep a resistance band in a circular shape on the ground
- Focus on switching multiple times for on the leg and then randomly when landing pivot on the lead leg cutting the angle
- The pivot can be to either side
