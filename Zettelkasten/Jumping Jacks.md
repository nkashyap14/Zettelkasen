# Jumping Jacks

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter how to move his feet quickly
## Textual Description

- Feet is in the first box
- Jump out
- Move forward into the next box
- Jump out
- Repeat
- Keep your hands up in fight position
- Don't turn around but go backwards
