# Step & Feint

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter a feint with the lead hand which allows you to set up a power punch
## Textual Description

- Step forwards while also moving your lead shoulder as if you are goign to throw a lead hand
- From there can follow up with a power punch with the rear hand
- In the [[Open Stance]] you can use this to also move forward get outside foot control and then follow up with a rear shot
