# Tenshin Hands Routine

## Goal of Drill

- Sharpen up [[Southpaw]] hands
## Textual Description

- Southpaw
- Move lead foot forward step jab bring back
- Sit a bit to pretend your jabbing to body fake with head as well jab high
- Bring back create distance 1, 2 then reset slightly
- Slight feint jab hand into 2 into lead upper into jab cross 40% feint a jab cross
- feint feint jab cross retract cross into rear upper 1 2
- jab step to the left rear upper to body step back slightly cross lead upper
- step pivot on lead foot jab cross
- step pivot jab
- guess range a bit, slip a 2
