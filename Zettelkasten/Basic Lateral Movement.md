# Basic Lateral Movement

## Goal of Drill

- To teach beginners brand new to boxing footwork a great way to drill a basic lateral movement pattern
## Textual Description

- Front foot ahead in the ladder back food behind in the ladder spot next to it
- Move right and left across the ladder. this time you aren't traversing the ladder forwards and backwards
- When moving right you push off with the left leg
- When moving left you push off with the right leg
- Basic mistakes to avoid
	- Having the backleg catch up to the front foot. The backleg should be in the ladder rung next to the one that the front leg is in at all times
