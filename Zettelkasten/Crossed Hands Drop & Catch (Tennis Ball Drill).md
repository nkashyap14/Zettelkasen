# Crossed Hands Drop & Catch (Tennis Ball Drill)

## Goal of Drill

- Improve eye hand coordination + hand speed + focus
## Textual Description

- Have two tennis balls in hands
- Cross your arms
- Drop the tennis balls
- Uncross your arms and catch both
