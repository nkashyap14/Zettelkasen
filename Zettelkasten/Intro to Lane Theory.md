
- Based on head slot there are a few punches that an opponent can hit you with quickly and powerfully
- 10 different head slots in boxing
- 5 lanes
	- Center lane
	- Cross lane
	- Open Lane
	- Jab Lane
	- Closed lane
- Two levels:
	- High
	- Low
- The combination of the two gives you 10 possible positions your head can occupy
	- Ex:
		- [[High open boxing lane]]
			- Your head is on the outside of opponents cross hand
		- [[High cross boxing lane]]
		- [[High center boxing lane]]
		- [[High Jab boxing lane]]
			- The below is done with the idea of a closed stance matchup where fighters are in the same stance
			- If at this head slot at range opponent will likely be throwing a 1 or a 2
			- This is when your head is lined up with the opponents jab
			- Focus on slipping either way
			- If you are in range then opponent will likely throw a 5 or a 4.
				- Lead upper or rear hook
				- Will need to slip or roll
			- The opponent could still throw other spots however this would be for increased risk and decreased reward
				- Ie opponent could throw a lead hook/3 but ti would have a big tell as it would need room to accelerate/might not have enough room to accelerate and it would ned up as a slap. You would be inside their defensive range and leaves them vulnerable to a 4 or a right hand
			- So defensive options at range at the high jab position are typically slips
				- Straight punches are fast so its hard to do it reactively
				- Its better to slip proactively.
					- But after you slip proactively you need to consider what options the opponent has to come back with
					- More likely than not opponent will follow up with the opposing hand!
					- Example if you slip open aka to your cross side in an attempt to slip their jab then one can expect a rear cross or rear hook. This can be dealt with by proactively rolling!
					- If you slip open and your opponent threw a cross then you are now sitting in their jab lane! Now you must either defend a 1 or a 5 aka a jab or a lead upper 
					- If you slip closed and your opponent threw a cross then roll proactively to deal with the incoming three
		- High closed
	- Same with low
		- Low Open
		- Low Cross
		- Low Center
		- Low Jab
		- Low Closed
