# Lead Jab & Lead Footwork Laterally

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter the basic movements on how to jab while moving laterally left and right
## Textual Description

- Step to the lead hand side and jab
	- Try and add a reset to between the movements so that you are resetting
	- This isn't a bounce jab rather your lead leg is in the rungs of the ladder the entire time and you are moving laterally
