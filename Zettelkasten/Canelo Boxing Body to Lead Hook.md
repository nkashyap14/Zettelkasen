# Canelo Boxing Body to Lead Hook

## Goal of Drill

- Teach yourself an effective lead hook that stems off a [[jab]] fake to the body alongside realistic movement
## Textual Description

- Jab to body
- Jab fake to opponents face
- Retract then jab to opponents body
- Immediately in a twitch movement bring it back up and around as a hook to opponents head
