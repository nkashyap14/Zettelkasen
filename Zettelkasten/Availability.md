# Availability

- Probability of a system to work as required when required
- Redundancy is a wildly used mechanism to achieve higher availability
- Redundancy being the storing of data in multiple computers
- Percentage of time that some service or infrastructure is accessible to clients and is operated under normal conditions
$$
A = \frac{(Total Time - Amount of time Service Was Down)}{Total Time} * 100
$$

- Availability is a ratio. The higher the value the better
- Typically measured as a number of 9's
- 1 9 = 90% uptime = 36.5 days downtime per year = 72 hours per onth = 16.8 hours per week
- 2 9's = 99% uptime = 3.54 days per year downtime = 7.20 hours per month = 1.68 hours per week
- 3 9's  = 99.9% uptime = 8.76 hours downtime per year = 43.8 minutes per month = 10.1 minutes per week
- 4 9's =  99.99% uptime = 52.56 minutes downtime per year = 4.32 minutes per month = 1.01 minutes per week
