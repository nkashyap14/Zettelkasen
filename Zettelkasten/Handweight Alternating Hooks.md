# Handweight Alternating Hooks

## Goal of Drill

- Increase ability for hooks with speed and power
## Textual Description

- Make sure to rotate rear leg when sending rear hook and focusing on solely hip rotating with the lead hook
- Make sure the hooks face and shoulder
