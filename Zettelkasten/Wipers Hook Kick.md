# Rotate & Pull Hook Kick

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- The goal of this drill is to increase internal rotation + open up the hips for the hook kick
## Textual Description

- Stand on right leg. Grab something for balance
- Point your leg out as in a split or something and then rotate from side to side in an semi circle as if you are wiping a wind shield
- Should change in elevation from side to side
- Similar to other exercises also set of 10 and do for multiple sets
