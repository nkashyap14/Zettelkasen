# In and Outs

## Goal of Drill

- To give a fighter increased rhythm + the ability to move in and out towards an opponent quickly
## Textual Description

- Have a resistance band on the ground
- Start with lead leg in the band
- Move the back leg into the lead leg position wwhile simultaneously moving the lead leg forward out of the band
- Then move the lead leg back into the original position while also moving the back leg back into the original position
