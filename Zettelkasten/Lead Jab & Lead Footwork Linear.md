# Lead Jab & Lead Footwork Linear

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter the basic movements on how to jab while moving forward and backwards
## Textual Description

- When stepping forward with the jab the lead leg steps forward when the jab is being thrown
- The back leg comes forward as the jab retracts
- When stepping back the rear foot moves first as the lead hand throws
- Then the lead foot moves back as the jab retracts
- Make sure the back foot doesn't come into the ladder at all.
