# Step & Pivot

## Goal of Drill

- To teach a fighter how to cut an angle
## Textual Description

- Step forwards and then angle off to the outside of the lead hand
- You're bringing your lead leg forwards and then the back leg is shifting around the lead leg into a different location pointing at your opponent
