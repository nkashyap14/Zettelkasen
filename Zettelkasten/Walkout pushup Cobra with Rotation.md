# Walkout pushup Cobra with Rotation

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Increased back mobility + spinal rotation + increased hip fleixbility
## Textual Description

- Walk out on your hands into pushup
- Go into cobra + rotate head/body both sides
- Walk back into downwards dog then go back into standing up
