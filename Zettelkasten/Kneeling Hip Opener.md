# Face Down Lumbar rotations

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- Hip flexors + groin
## Textual Description

- Put one knee on the ground and other leg posted almost on the same x axis to the side
- Move your hips forward and back this while transferring weight when back to your  hips and lower body and when forward to your hands
