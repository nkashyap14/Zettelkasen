# Hook Kick Basic Drill

## Goal of Drill

- Drill to practice hooking motion and build leg strength
- This is a progression drill to help the fighter progress towards a [[hook kick]]/[[Wheel kick]]
## Textual Description
- Put both hands on the ground. One knee in the ground. Chamber the other leg to your hip. Send the leg outwards straight then hook back
- Do for sets of 10
