# 90_90 Hip Rotation

## Goal of Exercise + Muscle Groups Worked

- To unlock hip mobility + warmup the body specifically hip flexors prior to a workout
## Textual Description

- Keep your legs on a 90 degree angle on the ground. one pointing forwarding and the other to your side
- Then rotate your hips in the other direction in such a way that the legs end up in opposite positions
- Make sure to not raise your hip while doing so
